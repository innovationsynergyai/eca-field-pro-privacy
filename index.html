East Coast Air — Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026 · App: East Coast Air (iOS) · Bundle ID com.northeastnoir.ecafieldpro

Who operates the app

The East Coast Air app is a business field‑operations tool published by Northeast Noir LLC and built by Innovation Synergy AI Inc for use by East Coast Air LLC's technicians (South Shore, Massachusetts). It is distributed to the company's staff, not to the general public. For support, privacy questions, or data requests contact Michael Joseph Bowen at michael@innovationsynergyai.com.

The app does not request your precise location, contacts or microphone. It contains no advertising, no third‑party analytics SDK, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers

Depending on how the company has configured the app, the following services may process data on the company's behalf. If a service is not configured, the related feature is unavailable and no data is sent to it.

  • Supabase — database hosting used to synchronise customers, jobs, proposals and agreements between the company's devices (United States).
  • OpenRouter / Anthropic — the optional “Atlas” assistant and photo analysis send the question you type, equipment text, pricing context and any photo you choose to analyse to a large‑language‑model service to generate a response. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, Atlas answers on‑device where possible.
  • HighLevel (GoHighLevel) — the company's CRM. The app reads contact and pipeline data from the company's CRM account to show account vitals and create jobs.
  • Open‑Meteo — weather for the South Shore service area, requested for a fixed service‑area coordinate (no device location).
  • U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics — public price‑index data used to adjust material cost estimates. No personal data is sent.

All requests use HTTPS. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users, investigate abuse or protect legal rights.

Storage and security

Records are cached in the app's private storage on your device and protected by iOS data protection. Integration credentials are held in the device Keychain. Depending on your Apple settings, device backups may include cached job data.

Retention and deletion

Customer and job records are retained while the company needs them for its operations and are deleted from the device when the app is removed. To request deletion or correction of records held in the company's database, email michael@innovationsynergyai.com. Customers of East Coast Air LLC may also contact the company directly.

Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete the records you create, choose not to use the Atlas assistant or photo analysis, and decline camera or photo‑library access in iOS Settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or a portable copy of personal information. Send requests to the contact above; we may need to verify your identity first.

Children

This is a workplace tool for licensed HVAC technicians and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We will update this policy when the app's practices change and revise the effective date above.
