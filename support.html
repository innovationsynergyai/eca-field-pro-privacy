Common questions

Atlas says it needs a connection. Atlas uses Apple Intelligence on supported devices, otherwise a network service configured by the company. Check your connection or ask your administrator whether the assistant is enabled.

Account Vitals shows zeros. The CRM integration is configured by the company. If it is not enabled, the dashboard shows local data only.

Where are my proposals and logs? Proposals appear in the Proposals tab. Coastal logs and Mass Save documentation are added to the job's notes in Job Detail and kept on the device.

Delete my data. Removing the app deletes on‑device data. For records in the company's database, email the address above.
